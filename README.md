### 🚀 About KatalisEdu
**KatalisEdu** is a modern, secure, and scalable **School Management & Student Information System (SIS)** designed to simplify and automate academic operations for schools, colleges, and training institutes.

Built upon the proven foundation of the **openSIS Community Edition**,  
KatalisEdu introduces:
- a **refreshed modern interface**,  
- a **custom authentication system**,  
- an **independent database architecture**, and  
- multiple **new smart modules** crafted for the next generation of educational management.

> “Katalis” means **catalyst** — our mission is to accelerate educational efficiency through technology ⚡

---

### 🌟 Key Features

- 🎓 **Student Information Management** — Complete student profiles, history & attendance  
- 👩‍🏫 **Staff Management** — Secure role-based permissions & staff records  
- 🏫 **Institution Management** — Manage one or multiple campuses  
- 📚 **Course & Subject Control** — Streamlined curriculum & course mapping  
- 🗓️ **Smart Timetable & Scheduling** — Dynamic, drag-and-drop style scheduling  
- ✅ **Attendance & Reporting** — Daily/period-wise logs with analytics  
- 🧮 **Gradebook & Results** — Built-in grade calculations, progress & transcripts  
- 📢 **Announcements & Communication** — Internal messaging & notices  
- 📊 **Reports & Dashboards** — Visual insights and export-ready reports  
- 🔒 **Enhanced Security** — Isolated school databases & secure access tokens

### 🛠️ Installation

- Apache 2.4+  
- MySQL 5.7 / 8.0 or MariaDB 10.4+  
- PHP 8.x
